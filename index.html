HELLO!

Hi there! I'm a Master's student at IIT Roorkee, with a keen interest in astrophysical phenomena through computation. Currently, the problem of planet formation appeals to me. It is not completely a mystery, but the theoretical models behind protoplanetary discs — construction zones for planetary systems — make use of all kinds of physics.

In general, I like falling into the rabbit hole of chaotic problems and digging through them for a messy, yet elegantly underlying solution which computational techniques may reveal.

My current focus is on applying fluid dynamics to model the evolution of dust within protoplanetary disc structures, particularly the role of turbulence in the growth, settling and concentration of solids on their way towards planetesimals.

Prior to this work, I explored variable stars through observational techniques at the Aryabhatta Research Institute of Observational Sciences (ARIES), analysing photometric data and developing pipelines for period detection. Although interesting while it lasted, the experience made me realise that I prefer the theoretical and computational side of astronomy much more than the data-analysis side.
