Introduction

About me

I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India. My academic interests lie at the intersection of astrophysical fluid dynamics, numerical modelling and planet formation.

I enjoy working with computational methods to investigate physical processes in astrophysical environments. I am particularly interested in understanding the role of turbulence in shaping the evolution of solids in protoplanetary discs.

Current work

Research projects

M.Sc. Research Project

Turbulence of Dust in Protoplanetary Discs

Investigating the evolution of dust in protoplanetary discs, with an emphasis on the competition between coagulation, fragmentation and turbulent transport. Developing a vertically resolved, multi-species dust coagulation model to study how the vertical structure of a disc influences grain growth.

Dust coagulation Turbulence Numerical modelling ODEs
Observational Astronomy

Variable Stars in NGC 2506

Developed a differential photometry pipeline for time-series observations obtained with the 3.6-m Devasthal Optical Telescope. Applied Lomb–Scargle periodograms and Box Least Squares to investigate stellar variability, with classifications supported by TESS and Gaia data.
