Previous research & projects

DEC 2025 — FEB 2026

Variable stars in NGC 2506 · ARIES

Under Dr. Yogesh Chandra Joshi, I worked with multi-epoch optical observations from the 3.6-m Devasthal Optical Telescope. I developed a differential-photometry pipeline and used Lomb–Scargle and Box Least Squares methods to search for periodic variability, followed by cross-matching with TESS and Gaia catalogues.

Photometry pipeline on GitHub ↗

JUN — JUL 2025

Cosmological parameters from Type Ia supernovae

At India Space Academy, I analysed the Pantheon Type Ia supernova dataset using numerical fitting and residual analysis to study cosmological parameters and the age of the Universe. My ΛCDM fit gave H₀ = 72.97 ± 0.26 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ and Ωₘ = 0.351 ± 0.019 in that analysis.

Analysis on GitHub ↗

JAN — MAY 2025

Quantum algorithms for cryptography

For my B.Sc. thesis at Gautam Buddha University, I developed Python-based simulations of quantum algorithms for cryptographic applications using Qiskit, including work related to the BB84 quantum key distribution protocol.

Project on GitHub ↗

AUG 2026 — PRESENT

Handheld solar irradiance and UV-index detector

An independent instrumentation project involving an ESP32, LTR390 UV sensor and AS7341 spectral sensor. I am working on sensor acquisition, calibration, irradiance estimation and a portable enclosure. The device is being developed for use in the IIT Roorkee Atmospheric Physics Lab.
