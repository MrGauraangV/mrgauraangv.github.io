About me

I'm currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. My interests are centred on computational astrophysics and numerical modelling, particularly problems connected to star and planet formation.

What draws me to planet formation is the range of physics that comes together in a protoplanetary disc: gas dynamics, aerodynamics, settling, turbulent transport and collisions between dust grains. I enjoy problems where the physical picture is complicated, and where a combination of analytical reasoning and numerical work can make that complexity more understandable.

My route into this area also included observational astronomy. At ARIES I worked on variable stars using optical time-series observations. That experience gave me practical exposure to astronomical data and time-series methods, while clarifying that I am more drawn to theoretical and computational questions.
