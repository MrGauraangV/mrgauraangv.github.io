Dust growth and vertical settling in protoplanetary discs

M.Sc. thesis · Supervisor: Dr. Akshay Bhatnagar, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee

I'm investigating the vertical evolution of dust in protoplanetary discs, focusing on the interplay between aerodynamic coupling, gravitational settling, turbulent diffusion and particle growth.

So far, I've derived analytical solutions for dust growth during laminar vertical settling and for steady-state dust distributions under competing settling and turbulent diffusion. I've numerically integrated particle trajectories and grain-growth equations using Epstein drag, and compared the numerical results with analytical predictions.

One aspect I have been examining is the height dependence of the Epstein stopping time and how it changes the vertical concentration of dust. The calculations indicate that accounting for this height dependence produces a more concentrated dust layer than the constant-stopping-time approximation.

I'm now extending the model into a vertically resolved, multi-species dust-coagulation framework, with the aim of studying grain growth under turbulent relative velocities and gas dynamics.
